# GitCompass 0.2.6 (beta)

## What's new: easier words everywhere

- **Everyday words first, Git words in brackets.** For example *Save a version (commit)* and *Set work aside (stash)*. You learn the real words without needing them on day one.
- **Clearer problems.** When something goes wrong you see *What happened* and *What to do*. There are new explanations for: no saved versions in this folder, Git busy, nothing to save, can't reach the online copy, and Git needing your name.
- **Empty screens tell you what to do next.** This covers no projects yet, no changed files, nothing chosen to save, and no history yet.
- **Tooltips for the access modes** (Read-only, Monitor only, Allow changes) and for *Check team updates*.
- **A "Words you'll see" table** in the README. It comes from the same word list the app uses.
- A full list of what we checked is in [docs/beginner-audit.md](../beginner-audit.md).

Nothing about safety changed: every change is still previewed and needs your OK.

Requirements, checksum steps and known limitations are unchanged from [0.2.0](0.2.0.md).
