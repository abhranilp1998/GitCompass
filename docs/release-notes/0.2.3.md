# GitCompass 0.2.3 (beta)

## What's new

- **Mission 1 now means it.** "See a change" completes when you actually open the **welcome.txt** diff in Changed files. Opening the page, or another file, doesn't count.
- **Progress bar.** The missions page shows a "Mission X of 5" bar.
- **A small celebration for each mission.** Finishing a mission shows a ✓ message explaining what you just did (for example "Saved! That version now lives in your project's history.") and what comes next.

Requirements, checksum steps and known limitations are unchanged from [0.2.0](0.2.0.md) and [0.2.2](0.2.2.md).
