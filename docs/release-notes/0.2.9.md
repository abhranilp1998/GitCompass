# GitCompass 0.2.9 (beta)

## New: a welcome screen on first launch
- Explains what GitCompass does in three plain sentences.
- Asks one question, "How much do you know about Git?":
  - **I'm brand new**: the full walkthrough, with practice missions.
  - **I know a little**: just the key steps.
  - **I know Git**: a quick quiz, then skip ahead.
- Works with the keyboard and screen readers, and follows your theme.

## New: start over any time
- **Replay walkthrough** in Settings, Learn and the F1 help window.
- **Show welcome screen again** in Settings and Learn.

## Notes
- If you already used GitCompass, you won't see the welcome screen automatically. Open it from Settings.
- Download `GitCompass-Setup-0.2.9.exe` (recommended) or the portable ZIP. Not code-signed yet: on "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
