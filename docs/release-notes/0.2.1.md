# GitCompass 0.2.1 (beta)

## What's new

- **Leaving the walkthrough now asks first.** **Exit walkthrough** opens "Leave the walkthrough? Your progress is saved" with **Resume** (the default; Esc does the same) and **Leave**.
- **Pause is instant and exact.** **Pause walkthrough** leaves straight away without asking. **Resume walkthrough** returns you to the same mission you paused on.

Requirements, checksum steps and known limitations are unchanged from [0.2.0](0.2.0.md).
