## Handoff reconciliation

The original **20 requirements (FR01–FR20)** and **38 scenarios (AT01–AT37 plus AT08a)** are all represented in these records. Implementation, core safety, terminal/reference, UI/UX, research/docs and packaging work are linked to evidence; no original ID was dropped during the interrupted session. Every remaining condition is identified above or in the scenario matrix, so an unfinished acceptance item cannot silently become a completion claim.

The remaining checks include human/native workflow and accessibility observation, live designated GitHub/transport policy, fresh-user/OS/install/update acceptance and publisher distribution decisions. Specific unexecuted controlled variants are also retained: possible post-server-acceptance interruption (AT18), restored-connectivity/authentication/dedup timing (AT28/AT36), and the proposed 250 ms visible-output latency measurement (FR10/AT21). These are acceptance gaps, not lost assignments. M5 and M7 remain open.
