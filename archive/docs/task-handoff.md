## Work intentionally still open

The app is an unsigned internal trial. Live GitHub App sign-in/submission needs a registered App and designated sandbox. Production signing/distribution, fresh-machine install/update, company OS coverage, manual keyboard/screen-reader/high-contrast/DPI checks and a real Git-beginner pilot remain release acceptance work. They are explicit items in [acceptance](../../docs/acceptance.md) and [pilot-checklist](../../docs/pilot-checklist.md), rather than unassigned implementation tasks hidden by a disconnect.

Controlled acceptance variants also remain explicit: interruption after possible server acceptance (AT18), restored authentication/connectivity and scheduler notification deduplication timing (AT28/AT36), and measurement of the proposed 250 ms output-display latency (FR10/AT21). Existing interruption, failed-fetch, process-output and notification-policy assertions do not automatically close these distinct variants.

The interface's nickname rows cannot be mapped reliably to these task names from a screenshot alone. The seven saved assignments and their current results are the audit trail used here; the exact meaning of a hover count versus a visible row count has not been established.
